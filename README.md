One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who has just been liquidated — written for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition "Fortune Cookie for the Liquidated Trader".

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes a single fortune, at most 20 words, to the entry output (`/output/entry.txt`, or the path given by the `ENTRY_OUT` / `ENTRY_OUT_DIR` environment variable).

The fortune:

> Your lucky numbers are 5, 25, and 100 — the leverage levels that liquidated you.

15 words, 80 characters.
